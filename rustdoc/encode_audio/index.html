Audio extraction and transcoding through FFmpeg libraries.

encode selects an audio-relative stream, decodes it once, and sends each decoded frame through independent filter/encoder branches. Dual mode muxes Opus followed by FLAC into Matroska without intermediate encoded files. Outputs are published without overwriting existing files only after the decoder, filters, encoders, and muxer have all finished successfully.
